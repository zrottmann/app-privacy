AI Mandarin Tutor

Gamified Mandarin & Cantonese learning for kids.

Effective: June 11, 2026

What we collect

No data collected This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with the developer. Everything happens on your device.

On-device data

All learning progress, vocabulary, and settings are stored only on your device. Microphone and speech recognition are used solely for the on-device pronunciation practice (“Speak & Score”) and are processed on-device; audio is not recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Third parties

This app uses no third-party services and makes no network requests with your data.

Children

This app is designed for children to learn Chinese and is safe for kids: it collects no personal information, has no ads, no in-app purchases, and no external links without a parent gate.

Your choices

Because most data stays on your device, you can remove it by deleting the app. Where an optional online feature is used, you can disable it or contact us to request deletion of any account data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above reflects the latest version.
