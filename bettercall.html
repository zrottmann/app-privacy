Better Call

An AI voice assistant that helps seniors get things done and place calls.

Effective: June 11, 2026

What we collect

This app collects the following, only to provide features you actively use:

  • Account email and name, if you create an account.
  • Calls you choose to place (the destination number and call metadata) to connect them.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. There is no third-party analytics or tracking.

On-device data

Conversations with the assistant run on your device using Apple Intelligence when available; speech-to-text is performed on-device. Your profile (name, preferences) is kept in your account so it persists across reinstalls.

Optional third-party services

These are used only when you opt in (e.g., by adding your own API key or signing in). When unused, the app stays fully on-device.

Appwrite. Account, profile, and app data are stored in an Appwrite backend so they sync to your account.

Twilio (when you place a call). When you ask the assistant to call a business, the destination is dialed through Twilio to connect the call.

xAI (fallback). On devices without Apple Intelligence, your messages are sent to xAI to generate the assistant's replies. Authentication uses a short-lived token; no API key is stored in the app.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

Because most data stays on your device, you can remove it by deleting the app. Where an optional online feature is used, you can disable it or contact us to request deletion of any account data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above reflects the latest version.
