Mail Decoder

Point your camera at official mail and get a private, offline plain-language explanation — the action, deadline, and money involved.

Effective: June 11, 2026

What we collect

No data collected This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with the developer. Everything happens on your device.

On-device data

Everything happens on your device. The photo you take of a letter, the text recognition (OCR) that reads it, and the Apple Intelligence that explains it all run entirely on-device. The letter image, its text, and the explanation never leave your phone — nothing is uploaded, recorded, or shared. The app works fully offline and requires no account.

Third parties

This app uses no third-party services and makes no network requests with your data.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

Because most data stays on your device, you can remove it by deleting the app. Where an optional online feature is used, you can disable it or contact us to request deletion of any account data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above reflects the latest version.
