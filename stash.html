Stash

A gamified augmented-reality home-inventory and collectibles app.

Effective: June 11, 2026

What we collect

No data collected This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with the developer. Everything happens on your device.

On-device data

Your inventory, photos, and collection are stored only on your device. The camera and AR features process the live scene on-device to detect and card your items; background removal and cartoon styling run on-device.

Optional third-party services

These are used only when you opt in (e.g., by adding your own API key or signing in). When unused, the app stays fully on-device.

Grok / xAI (optional). If you add your own xAI key, a photo you scan may be sent to xAI for item recognition. Only when you scan with that option enabled.

Replicate (optional). If you add your own Replicate key and tap “Cloud Cartoonize,” that image is sent to Replicate for stylization.

eBay (optional). If you use the eBay listing path and sign in, listing details you enter are sent to eBay to create your listing.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

Because most data stays on your device, you can remove it by deleting the app. Where an optional online feature is used, you can disable it or contact us to request deletion of any account data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above reflects the latest version.
