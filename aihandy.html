AiHandy

AI home-project quotes and contractor-bid negotiation, powered by on-device Apple Intelligence.

Effective: June 11, 2026

What we collect

No data collected This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with the developer. Everything happens on your device.

On-device data

Your project descriptions, quotes, and photos are processed on your device using Apple Intelligence (Foundation Models) and the Apple Vision framework. Photos you add are analyzed on-device by default; nothing about your projects is collected by the developer.

Optional third-party services

These are used only when you opt in (e.g., by adding your own API key or signing in). When unused, the app stays fully on-device.

xAI (optional). If you add your own xAI API key for higher-quality photo descriptions, the photo you ask to describe is sent to xAI. The on-device path is the default and requires no key.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

Because most data stays on your device, you can remove it by deleting the app. Where an optional online feature is used, you can disable it or contact us to request deletion of any account data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above reflects the latest version.
