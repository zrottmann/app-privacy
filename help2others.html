Help2Others

A gamified, pay-it-forward kindness app and community.

Effective: June 11, 2026

What we collect

This app collects the following, only to provide features you actively use:

  • With the optional online community enabled: your chosen display name, chosen emoji avatar, and kindness-activity counts.
  • Optionally, an email and name if you create a named account.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. There is no third-party analytics or tracking.

On-device data

By default the app runs fully on your device — your kindness log, level, and chain are stored locally, and the community appears in a simulated mode. Microphone and speech recognition power the optional consent-based listening feature and are processed on-device.

Optional third-party services

These are used only when you opt in (e.g., by adding your own API key or signing in). When unused, the app stays fully on-device.

Appwrite (optional). If online multiplayer is enabled, your display name, emoji, and kindness counts are stored in an Appwrite backend so other players can see the shared leaderboard and chains.

xAI (optional). If you add your own xAI key, a kindness summary you request is generated by xAI; otherwise an on-device template is used.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

Because most data stays on your device, you can remove it by deleting the app. Where an optional online feature is used, you can disable it or contact us to request deletion of any account data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above reflects the latest version.
