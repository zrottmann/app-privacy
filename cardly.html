Cardly

Design a greeting card with AI, then have it printed and mailed as a real folded card.

Effective: June 11, 2026

What we collect

This app collects the following, only to provide features you actively use:

  • The recipient's name and mailing address, when you choose to print and mail a card.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. There is no third-party analytics or tracking.

On-device data

Your card designs, drafts, and message text are kept on your device. A recipient's mailing address is used only to address and send the card you ask us to mail; it is not linked to your identity and is never used for advertising or tracking.

Optional third-party services

These are used only when you opt in (e.g., by adding your own API key or signing in). When unused, the app stays fully on-device.

Print &amp; mail vendor (when you mail a card). When you tap to send a physical card, the card artwork and the recipient's name and mailing address you enter are transmitted to our print-and-mail provider solely to print and post that card. This happens only when you choose to mail a card.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

Because most data stays on your device, you can remove it by deleting the app. Where an optional online feature is used, you can disable it or contact us to request deletion of any account data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above reflects the latest version.
